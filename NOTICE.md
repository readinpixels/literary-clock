# Quotes: sources and licence

The quotes in `literaryclock.koplugin/data/` are converted from the English
collection (`quotes/quotes.en-US.csv`) of
[cdmoro/literature-clock](https://github.com/cdmoro/literature-clock) by
Carlos Bonadeo.

That collection comes from
[JohannesNE/literature-clock](https://github.com/JohannesNE/literature-clock),
which grew out of
[The Guardian's reader-contributed literary clock](https://www.theguardian.com/books/table/2011/apr/21/literary-clock).
JohannesNE's collection is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 2.5 Generic License](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/),
and the quote data here is shared under the same terms: credit the sources,
no commercial use, share changes under the same licence.

As cdmoro's own notice says, no single verified licence covers every
quotation in the collection. Each quote remains the work of its author,
named next to it, and is included as a short excerpt.

Credit to The Guardian and its readers, to JohannesNE, to Carlos
Bonadeo and the other contributors, and to the authors of the books quoted.
